1. Create a free GitHub account and a new empty repository.
2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in EVERYTHING from this folder
   (including the hidden .github folder), then commit.
3. Open the "Actions" tab, wait ~1 minute for "Build plugin" to turn green.
4. Open that run, download the "EconomyPlugin" artifact (zip), unzip it -> EconomyPlugin.jar
5. Upload the jar in your FalixNodes plugin page and restart the server.
